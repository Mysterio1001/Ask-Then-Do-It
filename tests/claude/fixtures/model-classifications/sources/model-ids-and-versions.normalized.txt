normalization: Manually transcribed canonical-ID rules and examples from the rendered official page.

Claude 4.6 and later canonical IDs use claude-{name}-{major}[-{minor}] without a date.
Examples: claude-sonnet-4-6, claude-sonnet-5, claude-opus-4-6, claude-opus-4-7, claude-opus-4-8, claude-opus-5.
Before Claude 4.6, canonical model IDs include a snapshot date.
Examples: claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929, claude-haiku-4-5-20251001.
Short pre-4.6 names are convenience aliases and are not canonical IDs.
Dateless 4.6-and-later IDs are pinned snapshots, not evergreen aliases.
